# Scope and limitations

For finite Leontief donor coordination with positive budgets, nonempty supports and arbitrary nonnegative weights, the aggregate under best responses from zero converges to the unique Nash-welfare allocation for every fair schedule.

Updates read the current contributions of other donors. Fairness requires infinite participation of every donor. The theorem concerns the aggregate and gives no rate or convergence assertion for the individual contribution vectors.

No prior theorem covering arbitrary weights under every such schedule was located. Confidence in this bounded priority assessment is moderate.
