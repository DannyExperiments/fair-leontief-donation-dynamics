# Literature and priority for Q132

Search cutoff 26 November 2026. The original question is in [Greger, dissertation, Open Problem 5, p. 99](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1759811/onlts4hzyc7oqggwzeixzb01l.Matthias%20S.%20Greger.pdf#page=115).

No prior theorem covering arbitrary weights under every such schedule was located. Confidence in this bounded priority assessment is moderate.

Brandt et al. proved bounded-waiting convergence and the binary fair case. Milchtaich’s Theorem 3 and Example 9 give the potential and equilibrium characterization, including restricted-block optimality by restriction. Elsner–Koltracht–Neumann already use a first nonfixing update under fair activation for norm paracontractions. The present proof uses equality of aggregates on a maximizing slice; a norm-paracontraction representation of these donor updates was not established. The literature search included deterministic coordinate methods, free-steering and projection algorithms, and citing papers on donor coordination.
